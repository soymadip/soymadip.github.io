## Process Of Business Registration In India

Following is the 10-step process to legally register and start a business in India:

1. **Company Name Availability Verification:** Checking whether the proposed business name is available or already in use.
2. **Digital Signature Certificate (DSC):** Obtaining a digital signature for online registration and filing of documents.
3. **Business Incorporation Certificate:** Registering the business legally and receiving the official incorporation certificate.
4. **PAN and TAN Registration:** Applying for a Permanent Account Number (PAN) and Tax Deduction Account Number (TAN).
5. **GSTIN Registration:** Registering under the Goods & Services Tax (GST) for taxation purposes.
6. **Shops and Establishment / Trade License:** Obtaining permission from local authorities to conduct business activities.
7. **ESI and EPF Registration:** Registering employees for insurance (ESI) and provident fund (EPF) benefits.
8. **Contract Labour and Professional Tax Registration:** Completing labor law and professional tax formalities as required.
9. **Preparation of Important Documents:** Submitting identity proof, address proof, and business details to the relevant authorities.
10. **Commencement of Business:** After completing all registrations, the business can officially start its operations.
