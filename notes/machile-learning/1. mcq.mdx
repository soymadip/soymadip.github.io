### 6. Given the entropy for a split, E(after)=0.39 and entropy before split, E(before)=1. What is the Information Gain?

<small>
    a) 1 <br/>
    b) 0.39<br />
    c) 0.61<br />
    d) 2.56<br />
</small>

**Answer:** c) 0.61

**Support:** Information Gain = E(before) − E(after) = 1 − 0.39 = 0.61.
