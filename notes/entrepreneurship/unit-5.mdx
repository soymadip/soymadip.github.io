# Unit 5: Funding and Schemes

Securing capital is often the most significant hurdle for any startup. This unit covers the different methods of funding and the various support systems provided by the government.

## Equity Vs Debt Funding

When raising capital, entrepreneurs typically choose between giving away a piece of the business or borrowing money.

### Equity Funding

A method of raising capital in which a company issues shares of ownership to investors in exchange for funds.

- **Key Points:**
  - No need to repay the money.
  - You give up some control and ownership.
  - Investors are entitled to a portion of the company's profits and assets.

### Debt Funding

A method of raising capital where a company borrows money from investors or lenders with a commitment to repay the actual amount along with interest within a specific period.

- **Key Points:**
  - Interest payment is mandatory regardless of profit.
  - You keep full ownership of the business.
  - Can create financial pressure if revenue is low.

## Venture Capital (VC)

A venture capital fund is a pooled investment fund that provides capital to early-stage, high-potential startups and small businesses in exchange for equity (ownership shares).

- **How it works:** VC firms collect funds from wealthy individuals, banks, and insurance companies. They carefully study business ideas and market demand before investing.
- **Support:** Apart from finance, venture capitalists also provide technical guidance, managerial support, and business contacts.
- **Exit:** They earn profit by selling their shares when the company becomes successful through an IPO (Initial Public Offering) or acquisition.

## Bank Loans For Startups

Bank loans provide essential financial support to start and expand a business.

- **Advantages:**
  - **Ownership:** Ownership remains with the owner; you don't share profit with the bank.
  - **Repayment:** Flexible repayment in installments over a fixed period.
  - **Interest Rates:** Often have lower interest rates compared to private borrowings.
  - **Growth:** Funds can be used for equipment, raw materials, and expansion.

## Government Schemes For Startups In India

The Government of India provides several schemes to support the startup ecosystem:

1. **Startup India Scheme:** Provides tax benefits, easy registration, and support for patents and funding.
2. **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS):** Provides financial assistance for making products, testing ideas, and starting operations.
3. **Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS):** Provides investment support through venture capital companies.
4. **Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY):** Offers small business loans without security or collateral.
5. **Stand-up India Scheme:** Provides bank loans specifically to women and SC/ST entrepreneurs.
6. **Atal Innovation Mission (AIM):** Promotes innovation and helps startups through various programs.
7. **Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (CGSS):** Helps startups get loans easily by providing government guarantees to banks.
8. **CGTMSE Scheme:** Provides collateral-free loans to small businesses.
9. **SAMRIDH Scheme:** Specifically supports tech and software startups.
10. **PMEGP:** Helps people start small businesses with the help of subsidies.
11. **NIDHI-PRAYAS:** Funding for new inventions and prototypes.
12. **TIDE 2.0:** Supports startups in AI, robotics, and emerging technologies.

## MSME / SSI Registration Benefits

Registering as a Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise (MSME) offers several advantages:

- **Easy Bank Loans:** Access to loans at lower interest rates.
- **Collateral-free Loans:** Ability to get loans without giving security or property.
- **Government Subsidies:** Access to various financial supports and subsidies.
- **Protection from Delayed Payments:** Buyers must make payments on time to registered MSMEs.
- **Tax Benefits:** Various tax concessions and exemptions are available.
- **Lower Electricity Bills:** Possible concessions on electricity charges for registered units.
- **Business Growth Support:** Access to government schemes for expansion and development.
