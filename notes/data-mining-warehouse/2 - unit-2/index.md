# Unit 2: Data Mining

This section introduces Data Mining, the Knowledge Discovery in Databases (KDD) process, and various mining tasks and trends.

## Themes Covered

- **Data Mining Basics:** Definitions and its role in the KDD process.
- **KDD Steps:** Cleaning, integration, selection, transformation, mining, evaluation, and presentation.
- **Mining Tasks:** Predictive (Classification, Regression) vs. Descriptive (Clustering, Association).
- **Functionalities:** Outlier analysis, association rules, and trend identification.
- **Presentation:** IF-THEN rules and Decision Trees.
