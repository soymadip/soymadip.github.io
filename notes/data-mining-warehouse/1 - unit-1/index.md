# Unit 1: Data Warehouse

This section covers the core concepts of Data Warehousing, including its architecture, key features, and differences from operational databases.

## Themes Covered

- **Definitions:** What is a Data Warehouse and why is it used?
- **Features:** Subject-oriented, integrated, time-variant, and non-volatile data.
- **OLAP Operations:** Roll-up, Drill-down, Slice, Dice, and Pivot.
- **Schemas:** Star, Snowflake, and Galaxy Schema models.
- **Process:** Steps in designing and implementing a DW.
